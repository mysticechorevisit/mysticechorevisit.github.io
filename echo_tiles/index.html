About the game

Four tiles light up in a pattern. Watch carefully, then tap the tiles in the same order. Every round adds one more tile, so the pattern gets harder to hold in your head.

  • Simple one-finger play, a round takes under a minute
  • Each tile has its own shape, so it works for colour-blind players too
  • Works offline, small download, no sign-up
