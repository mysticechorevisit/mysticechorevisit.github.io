Quick Brain Games

Small Android games for a quick brain break. Easy to start, one-hand play, works offline. Beat your score and share it with #BrainTapScore.

Sky StackOne tap. Stack the blocks perfectly. How high can your tower go?
Colour RushTap the matching button before the shape drops. Quick one-hand reflex fun.
Tint TapThe word says one colour, the paint says another. Tap the right one, fast.
Echo TilesWatch the tiles light up, then repeat the pattern. How long can you remember?
Quick SumIs the sum right or wrong? Tap true or false before time runs out.
