About the game

The word says BLUE, but it is painted red. Which colour do you tap? Follow the rule at the top of the screen and tap before the timer runs out. The rule flips every 8 points and the clock keeps shrinking.

  • Simple one-finger play
  • Colour shapes help colour-blind players
  • Gets faster the longer you last
  • Works offline, small download, no sign-up
