About the game

A shape falls. Tap the button with the same colour and shape before it crosses the line. Sounds easy, until the shapes speed up.

  • One-hand reflex play, a round takes about a minute
  • Four colours, each with its own shape, so colour-blind players can play too
  • Three hearts, and you win one back every 10 shapes
  • Works offline, small download, no sign-up
