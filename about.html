About Quick Brain Games

Quick Brain Games is a small independent game developer based in India. We make simple, free Android games that are easy to pick up, play with one hand and work offline.

What we make

  • Sky Stack: Tap Tower Builder
  • Colour Rush: Shape Match
  • Tint Tap: Colour Word Game
  • Echo Tiles: Memory Game
  • Quick Sum: Mental Maths Game

How our games are funded

All our games are free to download and play. They show ads served by Google AdMob. There are no in-app purchases, no accounts and no sign-up.

Our approach

  • Short rounds that fit into a spare minute
  • Clear controls and shapes as well as colours, so colour-blind players can play
  • No personal information asked for or stored by us
  • Ads kept away from the play area
