Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email, contacts, location or account details. Game scores are saved only on your device. This website does not use cookies or analytics.

Advertising

Our games show ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and approximate location. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown and can change this from "Privacy settings" in each game. Anyone can reset their advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings under Google > Ads.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
