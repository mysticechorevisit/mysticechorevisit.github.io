About the game

Tap at the right moment to drop each block on the tower. Miss the edge and the overhang is sliced away, so the next block gets narrower. Land three perfect drops in a row and your block grows back.

  • One-tap controls, playable with one hand
  • Calm colours that shift as your tower climbs
  • Perfect-drop combos that reward precision
  • Works offline, small download, no sign-up
